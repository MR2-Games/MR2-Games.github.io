1. Overview

MR2-Games ("we", "us", "our") operates mobile games available on Google Play. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why, and how you can control it.

This policy applies to all MR2-Games applications, including TastyMeats and any future titles published under this developer account.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data for purposes beyond what is described in this policy.

2. Data We Collect

Depending on which features you use, the following types of data may be collected:

Automatically Collected

  • Device & Advertising ID – collected by Google AdMob for ad delivery (see section 4)
  • Crash logs & diagnostics – collected automatically to help us fix bugs
  • App performance data – e.g. frame rate, load times

With Your Consent

  • Gameplay analytics – level attempts, scores, power-up usage, session duration. Stored locally on your device. Only aggregated and anonymised data is used for game balancing.

When You Sign In

  • Google Play Games Player ID – a unique identifier assigned by Google to your Play Games account, used to save and restore your game progress across devices
  • Display name – your Google Play Games display name, shown in-game

When You Make a Purchase

  • Purchase history – a record of in-app purchases (e.g. boosters, "No Ads"), processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive your payment details.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, camera, or microphone data.

3. Google Sign-In & Google Play Games

Our games offer optional sign-in via Google Play Games Services. Signing in allows your game progress to be saved to Google's cloud and restored if you reinstall the game or switch devices.

When you sign in, we access your Google Play Games Player ID and display name. This data is used solely to identify your save data. We do not access your Google account email or any other Google account information.

Sign-in is optional. You can play without signing in; your progress will be saved locally on your device only.

Google's privacy policy applies to data processed by Google Play Games Services: policies.google.com/privacy

4. Advertising (AdMob)

Our games display ads served by Google AdMob, a service operated by Google LLC. AdMob may collect and use your device's Advertising ID and other identifiers to serve relevant ads and measure ad performance.

Types of Ads

  • Interstitial ads – shown periodically between game sessions
  • Rewarded ads – optional ads you can watch to receive in-game benefits (e.g. continue after a game over)

Your Choices

  • You will be shown a consent prompt before personalised ads are displayed (EU/EEA users)
  • You can opt out of personalised ads in your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation
  • You can purchase "Remove Ads" to permanently remove all advertising from the game

AdMob's privacy policy: support.google.com/admob

5. In-App Purchases

Some of our games offer optional in-app purchases (e.g. booster packs, "Remove Ads"). All purchases are processed securely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

We retain a record of which purchases you have made in order to restore them if you reinstall the app. This purchase history is stored by Google Play and can be accessed via your Google account.

Purchases are non-refundable except as required by applicable law or Google Play's refund policy.

6. Analytics

Our games include an optional, privacy-friendly analytics system. Analytics help us understand how players interact with the game so we can improve difficulty balancing and the overall experience.

Analytics are opt-in only. You will be asked for consent before any analytics data is collected. You can change your preference at any time in the in-game settings.

Analytics data collected (with consent) includes:

  • Level attempts, completions, scores, and star ratings
  • Power-up usage
  • Session start and end times
  • Number of matches and special pieces created

This data is stored locally on your device in JSON format. It is not transmitted to external servers. You can view or delete your analytics data at any time in the Privacy Settings screen within the game.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Local analytics data is stored on your device until you delete it. You can delete all locally stored data at any time through the in-game Settings → Privacy screen.

For data held by third parties (Google Play Games save data, AdMob, Google Play purchases), please refer to Google's data deletion tools: myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy

To request deletion of any data associated with your account, contact us at: mr2games.mobile@gmail.com

8. Children's Privacy

Our games are rated for general audiences (PEGI 3 / Everyone). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at mr2games.mobile@gmail.com and we will delete the information promptly.

9. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights:

  • Right of access – request a copy of data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification – request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure – request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to object – object to processing of your data for advertising purposes
  • Right to withdraw consent – withdraw your analytics or advertising consent at any time in-game

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at mr2games.mobile@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.
